A maze tower defense

CUBEDEFENSE

Maze the creeps. Upgrade your towers. Survive 30 levels.

Press Enter to start

How to play
  • Creeps walk from the purple portal to the red exit. Each one that escapes costs a life.
  • Towers block the path, so build a maze to keep creeps under fire for longer. You can't seal it completely.
  • Every 4th level is an air wave. Flyers ignore your maze and Ground towers can't hit them.
  • Upgrade towers to climb their tiers. A Construction Facility unlocks the Super and Advanced lines.
  • Keys: 1-6 build, U upgrade, S sell, Space pause, M mute, F full map. Wheel zooms, drag pans.

Inspired by the classic Cube Defense map.
